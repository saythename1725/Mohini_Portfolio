Projects

Multilingual VoiceOps Support Agent

RAG, generative AI, speech recognition, LLM tool calling, Gnani speech-to-text and text-to-speech

A voice support agent that talks with customers in several languages and resolves their issues using company policy. It retrieves the relevant policy first and answers only from that evidence, so replies stay accurate and auditable.

What it does

  • Transcribes the caller’s speech and replies by voice in their language.
  • Retrieves policy passages and writes evidence-grounded answers.
  • Looks up verified order details through LLM tool calling.
  • Escalates to a support ticket when the issue needs a human.

Design focus: grounded answers over free-form generation, verified data over guesses, and a safe handoff to people.
