Built an AI customer support agent that answers questions using company policy documents in English, Hindi and Hinglish. It supports voice input and spoken replies, handles follow-up questions using conversation context, and can look up verified orders or escalate requests to a human agent.

Key features

  • Retrieves relevant policy information and records the sources used.
  • Uses speech-to-text and text-to-speech for voice conversations.
  • Verifies customers before showing private order details.
  • Creates a support ticket for escalation and confirms handover only after successful creation.
  • Evaluated on 52 policy questions and 23 conversations, tracking answer quality, evidence retrieval, tool calls and latency.
